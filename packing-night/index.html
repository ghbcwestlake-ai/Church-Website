How to pack a shoebox

Packing Night · Monday, November 2 · 6:00 PM

Good Hope Baptist Church, 821 Sampson St., Westlake

1

Pick a boy or a girl, and an age group

Each box gets a shoebox label. Mark boy or girl, and check one age group:

2–4 years5–9 years10–14 years

Tap the label to see it full size.

2

Fill it

Start with a “wow” item like a soccer ball with a pump, a doll or a stuffed animal. Then fill the box with other fun toys, personal care items and school supplies. Check the “do not include” list.

Open the gift ideas as a PDFTo save or print
3

Pray for the child

Pray for the boy or girl who will receive your gift. You can add a personal note and a photo.

4

Bring $10 for shipping

Each box requires $10 for shipping. Please bring exact change.
